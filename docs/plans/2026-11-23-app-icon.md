# App icon: done's round check, on the Mac and the iPhone

Branch `feat/app-icon` · Worktree `../done-wt/app-icon` · Base `origin/main` @ cf52065 (2026-11-23)
Status: built; the owner asked for it and sees it before merging. Size: short plan.

## Outcome

The app's icon is done's own round task check, ticked (a filled blue circle with a white tick), on the Mac and on the iPhone.

## Evidence (verified, do not re-derive)

- The iPhone app's icons (`crates/app/gen/apple/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset`, 18 sizes) were still Tauri's default logo.
- The Mac icons (`crates/app/icons/*`, drawn from `crates/app/icon.svg`) were a blue tick with no circle.
- The task check in the app is a round checkbox in `--primary` (#2f5fe0) with lucide's check inside (`ui/src/components/task-row.tsx` `TaskCheck`).

## Change

- `crates/app/icon.svg`: the Mac icon, the round check on the existing white rounded plate. `crates/app/icons/*` generated from it with `tauri icon`, keeping only the files already tracked (tray.png untouched).
- `crates/app/icon-ios.svg`: the iPhone icon, the round check larger on a full square, because iOS rounds the corners itself and shows transparency as black. Each size in the icon set is drawn from it with `rsvg-convert -b white` at its exact pixel size, so none has an alpha channel.

## Tests

None: an image. Checked by eye at 1024 and 180 pixels, and with `sips` that the iPhone icons have no alpha.

## Verification

The Mac icon is seen in "done next" or the next release. Not verified here: the iPhone icon on a device (it needs the iPhone build).

## Out of scope

A dark-mode or tinted icon (iOS 18), a new menu bar icon.
